# App Store listing copy

Paste-ready for App Store Connect. Character limits are Apple's; counts are
checked in the notes beside each field.

Everything here is drawn from what the app actually does — no invented delivery
times, discounts or coverage. **Two things to confirm before pasting:** that you
deliver across Freetown (the copy says so), and that WhatsApp support is staffed
by a person (it says that too).

## Keywords (100 max, comma-separated, no spaces after commas)

```
perfume,fragrance,cologne,oud,attar,arabic,scent,freetown,sierra leone,eau de parfum
```

Deliberately excludes brand names (Armaf, Lattafa, Rasasi). Apple rejects
keywords using trademarks you do not own — they are fine in the description as
statements of what you stock, but not as keywords.

## Copyright

```
2026 Ahmad Bah
```

Apple's format is year + rights holder, with no © symbol (the store renders one)
and no URL.

It's the personal name because the developer account is
`SU9B6GWGBX (Ahmad Bah (Individual))`. Note that an Individual account also
publishes that legal name as the **seller** on the listing — customers see
"Ahmad Bah" under the app title, not "Borteh Sprays". Showing the shop name
instead requires enrolling as an Organization (D-U-N-S number, separate
account), which isn't worth doing mid-submission. The app name and icon are
unaffected.
